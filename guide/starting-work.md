# Starting Work: Pay, Tax & Labor Basics

What every student should understand before signing a first job/internship offer in Vietnam:
contracts, probation, how salary is taxed, and what gets deducted. Pairs with
[Career Preparation](/guide/career) and [Career Paths](/guide/career-paths).

:::caution Educational summary — not legal or tax advice
Figures and rules here are **simplified and approximate**, based on Vietnam's Labor Code 2019,
the Law on Social Insurance and the Personal Income Tax law. **Rates, thresholds and minimum
wages change** — always confirm the current numbers with an official source, your HR department,
or a professional. Treat this as orientation, then verify.
:::

## Before you sign

- **Contract types** — fixed-term (≤ 36 months) or indefinite-term. Get it **in writing**;
  verbal-only arrangements are risky.
- **Probation (thử việc)** — usually paid at **≥ 85%** of the full-job salary. Maximum length
  depends on the role:

| Role type | Max probation |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise managers | 180 days |
| Needs a college/university degree | 60 days |
| Needs intermediate/vocational training | 30 days |
| Other (simple) jobs | 6 working days |

:::tip Always clarify the offer
Ask three things explicitly: **(1)** is the number **gross or net**, **(2)** what the
**probation pay and length** are, and **(3)** whether **mandatory insurance** is paid from day
one. Get answers in writing.
:::

## Gross vs. Net — the #1 confusion

- **Gross** = total salary *before* deductions (what the contract usually states).
- **Net** = what actually lands in your bank account, *after* insurance and income tax.

```
Net = Gross − employee insurance (10.5%) − personal income tax (PIT)
```

A "20 million" offer can mean very different take-home depending on which one it is. **Always ask.**

## What's deducted from your gross

**1. Compulsory insurance — employee share ≈ 10.5%:**

| Insurance | Employee | (Employer also pays) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social insurance (BHXH) | 8% | 17.5% |
| Health insurance (BHYT) | 1.5% | 3% |
| Unemployment insurance (BHTN) | 1% | 1% |
| **Total** | **10.5%** | **≈ 21.5%** |

The employer's ~21.5% is **on top** of your salary — not taken from you.

**2. Personal income tax (thuế TNCN)** — see below.

## Personal income tax (PIT)

For a tax resident, PIT is **progressive** on *taxable* income:

```
Taxable income = Gross − insurance (10.5%) − deductions
Deductions ≈ 11,000,000 / month (self) + 4,400,000 / month per dependent
```

| Monthly taxable income (VND) | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| ≤ 5 M | 5% |
| 5 – 10 M | 10% |
| 10 – 18 M | 15% |
| 18 – 32 M | 20% |
| 32 – 52 M | 25% |
| 52 – 80 M | 30% |
| > 80 M | 35% |

- **Tax code (mã số thuế)** — register one; you can also **register dependents** to claim the
  4.4 M deduction each.
- **Tax finalization (quyết toán thuế)** happens annually — you may get a refund or owe a top-up.
- **Non-residents** are generally taxed at a flat **20%** on Vietnam-sourced income (different rules).

:::note Deduction amounts can change
The 11 M / 4.4 M personal deductions are the figures in force at the time of writing and have
been adjusted before. Check the current level when you calculate.
:::

## Rough worked example (illustrative only)

For a **20,000,000 VND/month gross**, single, no dependents:

1. Insurance: 20 M × 10.5% = **2.1 M**
2. Pre-tax income: 20 − 2.1 = **17.9 M**
3. Deduction (self): **11 M** → taxable = 17.9 − 11 = **6.9 M**
4. PIT (progressive): 5% on first 5 M (0.25 M) + 10% on next 1.9 M (0.19 M) ≈ **0.44 M**
5. **Net ≈ 20 − 2.1 − 0.44 = ~17.46 M**

:::caution
Numbers rounded for illustration. Use a current online PIT calculator or your HR for the exact figure.
:::

## What the insurance buys you

- **BHXH (social):** sickness, maternity, work-accident, and **retirement pension** later.
- **BHYT (health):** subsidized medical treatment at registered facilities.
- **BHTN (unemployment):** support and training if you lose your job.
- Contribution bases are **capped** (e.g. BHXH/BHYT at 20× the base salary; BHTN at 20× the regional minimum wage).

## Hours, leave & overtime (basics)

- **Standard hours:** ≤ 8 h/day and ≤ 48 h/week.
- **Annual leave:** typically **12 paid days/year** (more with seniority and for hard conditions), plus public holidays.
- **Overtime pay:** ≥ **150%** on normal days, ≥ **200%** on weekly rest days, ≥ **300%** on public holidays — with legal caps on total overtime.

## Checklist before day one

- [ ] Offer states **gross or net** clearly (in writing)
- [ ] **Probation** pay and length agreed in the contract
- [ ] Confirm **insurance** is paid and from when
- [ ] Register your **tax code** and any **dependents**
- [ ] Keep your **payslips** and contract copies
- [ ] Sanity-check your first net pay against an online calculator
