ACLAB is opening applications for new **undergraduate and graduate members** for 2026.
We are looking for students excited to build real intelligent systems — robots, AGVs,
custom PCBs, embedded devices and edge-AI applications.

<!-- truncate -->

## Who we are looking for

- Undergraduate research members (robotics, embedded, edge AI)
- Graduate researchers (MSc/PhD)
- Student hardware / PCB engineers

No prior research experience is required for undergraduate roles — curiosity and
willingness to learn hardware and firmware matter most.

## How to apply

See the [Join ACLAB](/join) page for positions, requirements and the application
process, or email us at **aclab@hcmut.edu.vn**.
