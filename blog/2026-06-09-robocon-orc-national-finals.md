The **Robocon ORC National Finals 2025–2026** wrapped up on **June 6–7, 2026** at the
Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education, bringing together **nearly 200 teams
from more than 100 schools** across Vietnam — a highlight of the country's growing STEM robotics
community.

<!-- truncate -->

## At a glance

- **When:** June 6–7, 2026
- **Where:** Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education
- **Scale:** ~200 teams from 100+ schools nationwide, from primary school through university
- **Format:** multiple divisions by age/experience; teams were judged on robot assembly and
  programming, tactics, on-the-spot problem solving, and teamwork
- **Season:** the finals capped a year-long path of online rounds and regional tournaments

The event was organized by the Institute for School Design Research (under the Ministry of
Education and Training), co-hosted with Phan Thiet University, and sponsored by **OhStem
Education** — one of [ACLAB's partners](/partners) in STEM and robotics competitions — together
with Lidinco.
