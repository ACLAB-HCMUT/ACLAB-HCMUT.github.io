This month's lab seminar covers **deploying quantized vision models on embedded
accelerators** for real-time quality control on the factory floor.

<!-- truncate -->

## Topics

- Model quantization and pruning for edge devices
- Running inference on Jetson with TensorRT
- Measuring latency, throughput and power on-device
- A walkthrough of our visual-inspection project pipeline

The session is open to all lab members and interested students. Slides and demo
code will be shared in the [Knowledge Base](/docs/intro) afterwards.
