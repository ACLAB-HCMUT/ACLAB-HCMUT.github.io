About ACLAB

The Advanced Computing Lab (ACLAB) at HCMUT is a research and engineering lab building intelligent systems across robotics, embedded systems, edge AI, IoT and hardware-software co-design.

Who we are

Mission, vision & approach.

🎯

Mission

Advance applied research and engineering in intelligent systems, and train engineers who can build real hardware and software end to end.

🔭

Vision

Be a leading university lab bridging robotics, embedded systems and AI — from chip and PCB to autonomous platforms.

🛠️

Approach

Hands-on, project-driven work. We design PCBs, write firmware, build robots and deploy AI on real devices.

Our story

History & milestones

Dec 2023

Began R&D focus in Embedded Systems, AI and Robotics.

May 2024

Started an R&D collaboration with Kinis AI, bridging academic research and practical technology.

2025

Supported OhStem in running robotics competitions and activities for students and young engineers.

Aug 2025

Competed in Bách Khoa Innovation: INNOTECH with the Low-Cost Smart Irrigation System – HCMUT.

Jun 2026

Partnered with Heineken Vietnam and HCMUT on a humanoid-robot pilot for industrial manufacturing.
