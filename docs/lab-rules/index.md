# Lab Rules & Safety

Rules and safety practices that keep the lab productive and everyone safe.

## General rules

- Keep your workspace clean; return tools and equipment after use.
- Log usage of shared/expensive equipment.
- Do not remove hardware from the lab without approval.
- Respect booking schedules for shared instruments.

## Electrical & soldering safety

- Power down before wiring; check polarity and voltage before applying power.
- Use proper ventilation when soldering; wear eye protection.
- Treat LiPo/Li-ion batteries with care — correct charging and storage only.

## Robotics & moving hardware

- Keep clear of moving robots, arms and AGVs during operation.
- Use an accessible emergency stop on powered platforms.
- Test new motion code at low speed first.

## Incident reporting

Report damage, hazards or injuries to a lab manager immediately.
