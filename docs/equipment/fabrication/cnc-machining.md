# CNC & Laser

**Subtractive** machines that cut material away to shape parts, and laser machines that cut or
engrave.

## CNC machines

| Machine | Cuts | Typical materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CNC router** | Large, lighter cuts | Wood, acrylic, foam, soft metals |
| **CNC mill** | Precise, rigid | Aluminum, steel, plastics |
| **CNC lathe** | Turned (round) parts | Metal/plastic rods |
| **Desktop CNC** | Small parts, PCB isolation milling | PCB, plastics, soft metals |

- **Workflow:** CAD → **CAM** (toolpaths) → G-code → machine. Choose the right bit/feeds/speeds for the material.

## Laser cutter / engraver

- **Cut & engrave** acrylic, wood, leather, paper; engrave (and some cut) metals depending on laser type.
- **CO₂ lasers** for non-metals; **fiber lasers** for metal marking.
- Fast for 2D panels, enclosures, front plates and labels.

## Related

- **Vinyl cutter** — stickers, stencils, flexible-film cutouts.

:::danger Serious hazards
- **Eye/skin protection** for lasers; never bypass interlocks; lasers need fume extraction (and can ignite material — never leave unattended).
- CNC: secure workpieces, keep clear of spindles/bits, wear eye protection, manage chips/dust.
- See [Lab Rules & Safety](/docs/lab-rules) and get trained before first use.
:::
