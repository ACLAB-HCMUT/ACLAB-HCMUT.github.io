Process

How to apply.

A simple, four-step process from application to onboarding.

1

Apply

Send your CV, transcript and a short note about your interests to the lab email.

2

Interview

A short technical conversation about your background and what you want to build.

3

Trial project

Work on a small starter task with a mentor to get hands-on with the lab workflow.

4

Onboard

Join a project team and start with the Member Onboarding guides in the Knowledge Base.
